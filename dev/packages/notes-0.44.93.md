- New: the import room. "Import a book" now sits in the bottom right of the main screen and opens a room of its own. It takes it one step at a time: first your book comes in exactly as it is, then you go through your manuscript, your characters, then the places and notes, and a last check shows every word came across. Split a long chapter at its headings or keep it whole, set deleted scenes aside, turn a cast list into one page per person or keep it as one document, and make pages for names that come up a lot. Stop at any step: "Sort the rest later" keeps your place, and the button shows what's still to sort. You can turn the guide off in the room or in Settings.
- New: fog of war, at Focus. The Fog button beside Focus / Detail / Lore hides the book except the chapter you're on and the next one. It asks where you're starting: your "needs doing" list (right-click a chapter › Needs doing…), where you left off, or story order. "Done for now" moves you on and ticks the chapter off, Look up finds a character or place without lifting the fog, and the fog lifts when your list is done.
