- Writing history has a Copy… button. Copy your words by day as a list for a message or Discord, a table that pastes into Excel or Google Sheets as columns, or a picture of the grid with your totals. Pick this week, this month, the last 12 months, a year or your own dates. Only dates and numbers are copied; your books are named only if you tick Split by book. On an iPhone or iPad, Share… sends it straight to Messages, Notes or another app, and on a PC or Mac Save as file… keeps it as a spreadsheet file.
